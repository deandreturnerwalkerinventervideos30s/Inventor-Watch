Charles B. Brooks: The Street Sweeper

Brooks patented the first self-propelled street-sweeping truck in 1896. He also invented the modern ticket punch that collects paper scraps!

Garrett Morgan: Safety First

Born right here in Ohio, Morgan invented the three-position traffic signal and the safety hood (an early gas mask) used to save miners.
